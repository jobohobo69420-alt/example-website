Our story

Since 2019, roasted in small batches a few times a week.

Millhouse started as a single espresso machine in the back of a bakery. We roast in small batches, a few times a week, so what's in your cup was picked, roasted, and poured within days of each other.

Everything on the counter is made close by: bread from the oven next door, milk from a farm twenty minutes north, and beans from growers we've worked with for years.
